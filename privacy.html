SaveNest Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

SaveNest is a personal savings tracker. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

Information we store

If you choose Google sign-in, SaveNest uses your Google account identity to authenticate you. Savings goals, deposit amounts, dates, preferences and backup data may be stored in Firebase services associated with your account so that your data can sync across your devices.

Google Drive backups

If you explicitly connect Google Drive, SaveNest requests permission to create and manage backup files created by SaveNest in your Drive. Drive backup is optional. You can revoke this access in your Google Account security settings.

How information is used

Your information is used to provide sign-in, save and synchronize your savings data, and create backups you request. SaveNest does not sell your personal information or use it for advertising.

Storage and security

Data is handled by Firebase and Google Drive according to their security and privacy practices. Security rules are intended to restrict each account to its own records. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You may stop using Google sign-in, revoke Drive access, or delete your savings data using app controls when available. You can contact the developer at hexodesignz@gmail.com for privacy questions or data deletion requests.

Children

SaveNest is not designed for children under 13.

Changes

This policy may be updated as SaveNest features change. The latest version will be published at this address.
